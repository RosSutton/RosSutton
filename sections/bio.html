About Ros.

I’m an executive and team coach supporting professionals and organisations navigating periods of challenge, transition, and growth. Clients include leaders in professional and financial services, alongside CEOs and leadership teams across charities, schools, and family businesses. They often come to coaching looking for space to think clearly, make sense of competing pressures, and move forward with greater confidence and perspective. With more than 20 years’ experience across consulting, leadership, and technology, including time at PwC, I bring both commercial insight and a deep understanding of the human dynamics that shape leadership..

I am an accredited senior EMCC coaching practitioner and hold the Professional Certificate in Team, Board and Systemic Coaching from Henley Business School. I am also accredited in Facet5 and Strengthscope, and integrate a range of approaches into my practice, including positive psychology, embodied techniques, and constellations. Alongside my independent coaching work, I coach senior leaders through the Level 7 Executive Leadership Development Programme delivered by Corndel Management School in partnership with Imperial College London, supporting leaders to strengthen their leadership capability, team effectiveness, and organisational performance.

At the heart of my work is the belief that leadership is not about having all the answers, but about developing the awareness, resilience, and curiosity to navigate complexity well. I create a supportive yet appropriately challenging space where individuals and teams can explore what’s really happening, uncover unhelpful patterns, and develop practical ways forward. I’m particularly energised by helping people strengthen relationships, lead through ambiguity, and build more connected and collaborative teams. What I value most about coaching is seeing people reconnect with their own capability and create meaningful change within the systems around them.
