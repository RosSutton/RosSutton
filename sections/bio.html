About Ros.

I’m an executive and team coach working with individuals and teams navigating challenge, transition, and growth. With more than 20 years’ experience across consulting, leadership, and technology – including leadership roles with PwC abd a tech scale-up - I bring commercial insight and a deep understanding of the human dynamics that shape effective leadership.

I'm an accredited senior EMCC practitioner, hold the Professional Certificate in Team, Board and Systemic Coaching from Henley Business School, and coach senior leaders through the Level 7 Executive Leadership Development Programme delivered by Corndel in partnership with Imperial College London.

My clients include leaders in professional and financial services, and CEOs and leadership teams across charities, education, and scale-up businesses. They come seeking space to think clearly, navigate complexity, and move forward with confidence. What I value most is seeing people reconnect with themselves - watching that ripple outward into stronger relationships, more collaborative teams, and greater ease leading through ambiguity.
