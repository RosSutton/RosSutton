About Ros.

I’m an executive and team coach working with individuals and teams navigating challenge, transition, and growth. With more than 20 years’ experience across consulting, leadership, and technology – including leadership roles within a tech scale-up and with PwC, I bring a blend of commercial insight and a deep understanding of the human dynamics that shape effective leadership to my coaching practice.

I am an accredited senior EMCC coaching practitioner and hold the Professional Certificate in Team, Board and Systemic Coaching from Henley Business School. I’m accredited in Facet5 and Strengthscope, and trained in coaching modalities including positive psychology, embodied techniques, and constellations. Alongside my independent practice, I coach senior leaders through the Level 7 Executive Leadership Development Programme delivered by Corndel Management School in partnership with Imperial College London.

My clients include leaders in professional and financial services, alongside CEOs and leadership teams across charities, education, and scale-up businesses. They come to coaching seeking space to think clearly, navigate complexity, and move forward with confidence. I tailor my balance of support and challenge to each individual style, helping clients reflect, explore, and grow. This leads to stronger decision-making, healthier team dynamics, and improved organisational performance. What I value most is seeing people reconnect with their capability and confidence, creating meaningful change within the systems around them. I’m particularly energised when clients share how they’ve strengthened relationships, found greater ease leading through ambiguity, and developed more connected, collaborative teams.
