About Ros.

I'm an executive and team coach working with leaders and teams navigating change, complexity, and personal transitions. I bring a systemic coaching approach - one that recognises you don't exist in isolation, but within organisations, relationships, and dynamics that shape how you lead.

With more than 20 years' experience across consulting, leadership, and technology - including leadership roles with PwC and a tech scale-up - I bring commercial insight and a deep understanding of the human and organisational dynamics that shape effective leadership.

My credentials include senior EMCC practitioner accreditation and hold the Professional Certificate in Team, Board and Systemic Coaching from Henley Business School. I coach senior leaders through the Level 7 Executive Leadership Development Programme delivered by Corndel in partnership with Imperial College London.

My clients sit across sectors - financial services, professional services, retail, infrastructure, charities, education, technology and scale-ups. Through coaching, they develop new clarity about themselves and the systems they exist within - clarity that helps them see what's really happening, builds confidence to sit with complexity, and lead with authenticity and ease. What I value most is watching this ripple outward: stronger relationships, more collaborative teams, and people who flourish.
