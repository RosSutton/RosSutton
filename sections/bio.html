About Ros.

I’m an executive and team coach working with individuals and teams navigating periods of challenge, transition, and growth. With more than 20 years’ experience across consulting, leadership, and technology – including leadership roles within a tech scale- up and with PwC, alongside 10 years running my coaching practice, I bring a blend of commercial insight and a deep understanding of the human dynamics that shape effective leadership.

I am an accredited senior EMCC coaching practitioner and hold the Professional Certificate in Team, Board and Systemic Coaching from Henley Business School. I am also accredited in Facet5 and Strengthscope, and integrate a range of approaches into my practice, including positive psychology, embodied techniques, and constellations. Alongside my independent coaching work, I coach senior leaders through the Level 7 Executive Leadership Development Programme delivered by Corndel Management School in partnership with Imperial College London, supporting leaders to strengthen their leadership capability, team effectiveness, and organisational performance.

My clients include leaders in professional and financial services, as well as CEOs and leadership teams across charities, education, and scale-up businesses. They typically come to coaching seeking space to think clearly, navigate complexity, and move forward with greater confidence. I tailor my balance of support and challenge to their individual style which clients report, help them reflect, explore, and grow. What I value most about coaching is seeing people reconnect with their own capability and confidence, creating meaningful change within the systems around them. I’m particularly energised when clients share how they have strengthened relationships, found a greater ease leading through ambiguity, and are developing more connected and collaborative teams.
