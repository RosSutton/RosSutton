My Approach

Where curiosity meets courage.

"Ros combines warmth with challenge in a way that creates real growth" — CEO, International Charity.

Most coaching advice says to define a tight niche. Mine is different — my strength is agility, breadth and courage. I work across industries and sectors, drawing on different approaches depending on the client, and bring fresh, curious, commercially-minded challenge to every session.

My clients — individuals and teams — often face challenging relationships, critical decisions, complexity and uncertainty. Sometimes coaching is simply a space to voice what can't be said elsewhere.

Coaching isn't friendship; it's support for developing insight, navigating change, getting through difficult times. Curiosity helps clients discover new possibilities; courage helps them act on them. Together with strong relationships, these build the confidence to steer through complexity and lead with authenticity.

My role isn't to give answers, but to help you find your own — creating impact felt in your wellbeing, your leadership, and the people around you.
