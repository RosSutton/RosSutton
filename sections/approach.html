My Approach

Where curiosity meets courage.

"Ros combines warmth with challenge in a way that creates real growth" — CEO, International Charity.

Most coaching advice says to define a tight niche. Mine is different — my strength is agility, breadth and courage. I work across industries and sectors, drawing on different approaches depending on the client.

My clients — individuals and teams — come facing challenging relationships, critical decisions, complexity and uncertainty. Sometimes coaching is simply a space to voice what can't be said elsewhere.

Coaching isn't about friendship; it's about facing what's really going on — what's led here, what's yours to control, and where things are out of balance. Curiosity opens up new possibilities; courage helps you act on them. That's what builds the confidence to steer through hard moments and lead in a way that feels like you.

My role isn't to give answers, but to help you find your own. My coaching creates a space to reflect — I adapt to what's emerging, and work with the system you're actually in, rarely tidy, always specific to you. The impact shows up in your wellbeing, your leadership, and your team's performance.
