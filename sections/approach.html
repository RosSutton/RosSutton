My Approach

Where curiosity meets courage.

"Ros combines warmth with challenge in a way that creates real growth" — CEO, International Charity.

I work systemically, coaching you as an individual or team, within the context of the systems you operate in. It’s often useful to explore the relationships, dynamics and context around you - your team’s living history, what drew you to join, the unspoken rules, the connections and disconnections. Through coaching you can make visible what's invisible.

But here's what sets this apart: I hold three things equally. Your personal wellbeing. The wellbeing of those around you. And the commercial outcomes you're trying to achieve. A triangle that enables strength and growth.

I bring somatic awareness - noticing what's keeping you stuck not just in your head, but in your gut, your nervous system. Head gives insight. Heart creates connection. Gut builds trust. My approach is flexible and agile: whether you're navigating personal transition, leading teams through change, or building organisational agility, it’s about what's needed by you, right now.

The power of our coaching space can be felt when we slow down. It can take courage to slow down, to meet what's really happening, not rushing to fix it. Staying curious rather than judging. Finding clarity and naming your simple truths. Reinforcing the confidence to lead and flourish amid complexity.
